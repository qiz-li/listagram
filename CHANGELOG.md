# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-04)
First public release.

- Hides the Reels feed, Explore and search, the recommended home feed, and suggested accounts.
- Allows one reel per tap, and blocks swiping to the next one, including from reels opened in chats.
- Keeps messages, stories, friends' posts and reels, profiles, activity and follow requests.
- The block screen and "Friends' posts" button follow Instagram's light or dark theme.
